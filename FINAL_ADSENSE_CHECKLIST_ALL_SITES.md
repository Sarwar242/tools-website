## 💡 Pro Tips for Higher Approval Chances

### **1. Don't Rush**
- Wait 1-2 weeks for traffic to build
- Let Google index your pages
- Show that real people use your sites

### **2. Generate Legitimate Traffic**
- Share genuinely on social media
- Don't buy traffic or use bots
- Focus on quality visitors who will use the tools

### **3. Keep Sites Active**
- Publish new blog posts regularly
- Update content occasionally
- Show that sites are actively maintained

### **4. Be Patient During Review**
- Don't submit multiple times
- Don't make major changes during review
- Check email regularly
- Response time: 1-2 weeks typically

### **5. If Rejected**
- Read the rejection reason carefully
- Fix specific issues mentioned
- Wait 2-4 weeks before resubmitting
- Don't give up - many sites get approved on 2nd or 3rd try

## 🚨 AdSense Policy Reminders

### **NEVER Do These:**
- ❌ Click your own ads
- ❌ Ask others to click ads
- ❌ Use bots or automated traffic
- ❌ Buy traffic from shady sources
- ❌ Have too many ads (keep it reasonable)
- ❌ Hide or mislead about ad content
- ❌ Copy content from other sites
- ❌ Have prohibited content (adult, illegal, etc.)

### **ALWAYS Do These:**
- ✅ Provide real value to users
- ✅ Keep content original and high-quality
- ✅ Be transparent about ads (Privacy Policy disclosure)

- ✅ Respect user privacy
